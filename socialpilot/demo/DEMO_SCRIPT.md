## 1. 5-Minute Demo Script

**Audience:** Small business owner browsing online, short attention span
**Goal:** Generate interest → Book a full demo
**Format:** Screen recording or live walkthrough

### 0:00–0:30 — Hook

**You:** "How many sales opportunities do you lose every day because you can't respond to every comment and DM on Instagram?"

**[Pause for reaction]**

"Our beta customers were averaging 40% response rates. After MetaPilot? 100% response — in under 30 seconds. Here's how."

### 0:30–1:30 — Connect

**[Share screen — navigate to metapilot.ai, login, show dashboard]**

**You:** "This is the dashboard. Clean, simple. One click to connect Instagram — watch."

**[Click "Connect Instagram" → OAuth dialog appears → Authorize]**

**You:** "That's it. No webhooks, no developer setup, no API keys. Your AI agent is now watching your social accounts."

### 1:30–3:00 — AI in Action

**[Navigate to Inbox page — show real conversations]**

**You:** "Here's what happens next. A customer comments on your post: 'How much does this cost?'"

**[Click on the conversation — show AI-generated reply]**

"Our AI instantly classifies this as a pricing question — high purchase intent. It generates a reply using YOUR actual pricing from your knowledge base. And if the lead scores above 70? It auto-creates a CRM deal."

**[Click to Leads page, show a qualified lead]**

**You:** "Lead captured. CRM deal created. All before your morning coffee."

### 3:00–4:00 — Results

**[Navigate to Insights page]**

**You:** "Elite Dental Clinic — one of our beta customers — went from 5 leads a week to 50+. Their AI response rate is 100%. They spend 10 minutes a day reviewing responses instead of 2 hours manually typing."

| Response rate | 40% | 100% |
| Leads/week | 5 | 50+ |
| Time spent/day | 2 hours | 10 min |
| Revenue attributed | Manual tracking | AI-tracked +340% |

### 4:00–5:00 — Close

**You:** "You can have this running in 5 minutes. Free trial — no credit card. Want to see it work with YOUR business?"

**[Pause for response]**

"Let's book a 15-minute setup call. I'll walk you through connecting your Instagram and uploading your pricing. By the end of the call, your AI sales agent will be live."

## 2. 15-Minute Sales Demo

**Audience:** Serious buyer considering purchase
**Goal:** Convert to paid subscription
**Format:** Live walkthrough with their business context

### Segment 1 — Discovery (3 min)

**You:** "Before I show you the product, tell me about your business."

**Questions to ask:**
1. "How many social media accounts do you manage?"
2. "How many customer inquiries do you get per day via comments/DMs?"
3. "Who handles those conversations today?"
4. "What's the response time like?"
5. "How are you tracking leads right now?"
6. "What's your biggest frustration with social media sales?"

**[Take notes — reference them during the demo]**

### Segment 2 — The Problem Frame (2 min)

**You:** "Here's what we hear from every business owner. Social media is your biggest sales channel — but it's also your biggest time sink."

**[Share stats relevant to their industry]**

- "Restaurants: 60% of customers expect a reply within 10 minutes to a DM. Most restaurants reply in 4+ hours."
- "Clinics: 1 in 3 patients books through social media now. Each missed message = a lost appointment."
- "Salons: Your Instagram comments ARE your walk-in traffic. If someone asks 'Do you have slots today?' and you reply 6 hours later — they've already gone elsewhere."

**You:** "You're losing revenue right now. Not because your service isn't good — but because you physically can't be online 24/7."

### Segment 3 — Product Demo (7 min)

#### Step 1: Onboarding (1 min)

**[Walk through onboarding: business name → industry → connect Instagram]**

**You:** "Five minutes to set up. Let me show you."

#### Step 2: Knowledge Base (1 min)

**[Upload a real file if they have one — or use the demo clinic data]**

**You:** "Upload your pricing, your services, your policies. This is where your AI learns YOUR business. Not generic responses — YOUR prices, YOUR tone, YOUR voice."

#### Step 3: AI Auto-Reply (2 min)

**[Show a real comment and the AI-generated response]**

**You:** "Comment comes in. AI classifies it. Generates a reply. If it detects purchase intent — it creates a lead. Watch the CRM populate in real time."

#### Step 4: CRM Pipeline (1 min)

**[Navigate to CRM page]**

**You:** "Every lead becomes a deal in your pipeline. You can drag through stages — new lead, qualified, proposal, won. Revenue is tracked automatically."

#### Step 5: Approval Mode (1 min)

**[Toggle on Approval Mode in Settings]**

**You:** "Want to review every response before it goes out? Turn on Approval Mode. High-confidence replies go live automatically. Anything the AI is unsure about? You approve it first."

#### Step 6: Analytics & Reports (1 min)

**[Show Insights, Growth, Reports pages]**

**You:** "See how many leads you captured this week. How many deals you closed. How much revenue your AI agent generated — with AI attribution, so you know exactly what's working."

### Segment 4 — ROI Calculation (2 min)

**[Use their numbers]**

**You:** "Let's run the math for your business."

**Template calculation:**
- Your monthly visitors/comments: [their number]
- Typical conversion rate: 5-10%
- If AI captures just [X] more leads/month at your average deal value of [Y]
- That's ₹[X × Y] in additional revenue
- MetaPilot costs ₹[plan price]/month
- ROI: [Calculation]x in the first month

**You:** "This pays for itself in the first week."

### Segment 5 — Objections & Close (1 min)

**You:** "Common concern — 'Will the AI get it wrong?' That's why we built Approval Mode. You stay in control. Every response is logged. You can override anytime."

**You:** "Ready to get started? Let me set you up with a 14-day free trial — I'll personally walk you through the setup."

## 4. Common Objections & Responses

### Objection 1: "The AI won't understand my business"

**Response:** "Actually, that's the best part. You train it. Upload your pricing PDF, your service menu, your FAQ. The AI reads those documents and learns YOUR business — not generic answers. Plus you can review any response before it goes live with Approval Mode."

### Objection 2: "It's too expensive"

**Response:** "Let's run the numbers. If you capture just one extra customer per month through faster responses — that covers the subscription. Most of our customers see 10x+ ROI in the first month. The question isn't 'Can I afford it?' — it's 'Can I afford to keep losing leads to slow responses?'"

### Objection 3: "My customers want to talk to a real person"

**Response:** "And they will — when they're ready to buy. MetaPilot handles the initial conversation: answers pricing questions, checks availability, qualifies the lead. When it detects a hot lead, it flags it for you to take over. Your customers get instant answers, you get qualified leads. Win-win."

### Objection 4: "I don't have time to set this up"

**Response:** "Setup takes 5 minutes. I'll walk you through it right now. Connect Instagram → upload your pricing → done. Your AI agent is live. And after that, you spend just 10 minutes a day reviewing responses. Compare that to the 2+ hours you're spending now."

### Objection 5: "What if the AI makes a mistake?"

**Response:** "Three safeguards: 1) You can enable Approval Mode so every response needs your OK before going live. 2) Every response is logged — you can see exactly what the AI said. 3) You can override or edit any response instantly. Plus, our AI gets smarter over time as it learns from your corrections."

### Objection 6: "I already have a chatbot"

**Response:** "Chatbots are reactive — they sit on your website waiting for visitors. MetaPilot is proactive — it monitors your social channels, detects incoming conversations, and responds automatically. Plus it captures leads, updates your CRM, and tracks revenue. It's not a chatbot — it's an AI sales agent."

### Objection 7: "It won't work for my industry"

**Response:** "We work with [their industry] businesses every day. Let me show you examples from [similar business]. [Navigate to relevant industry example below or pull up a customer story.] The core workflow is the same: customer asks a question → AI responds with your info → lead gets captured. It works for any business with a social media presence."

### Objection 8: "I'm worried about security"

**Response:** "Your data stays yours. We encrypt everything in transit and at rest. We never use your customer conversations to train our models. Your business data — pricing, products, policies — is never shared. SOC 2 compliant infrastructure."

### Objection 9: "I want to think about it"

**Response:** "I completely understand. Here's what I'd suggest — let's start your 14-day free trial right now. You can see it work with your actual Instagram account. If it's not for you, cancel anytime — no charge. What do you have to lose?"

### Objection 10: "My team won't use it"

**Response:** "That's why we built it to be effortless. Your team doesn't need to 'use' anything — the AI works automatically in the background. Your sales team just sees leads appearing in their CRM. Your marketing team sees faster response rates. No training, no adoption curve."

## 5. Industry-Specific Examples

### Hotels & Resorts

**Use case:** Booking inquiries, room availability questions, amenity queries, check-in info

**Sample conversation:**
> **Guest DM:** "Do you have a pool view room available for next weekend?"
> **AI Reply:** "Thank you for reaching out! Yes, we have pool view rooms available next weekend. Our Premium Pool View rooms are ₹8,500/night including breakfast. Would you like me to check specific dates? We also have a special weekend package that includes dinner."

**Lead capture triggered:** When guest asks about availability or pricing
**Follow-up:** Send booking link, upsell packages

**Key selling points:**
- "Never miss a booking inquiry at 2 AM"
- "Respond to room availability questions in under 30 seconds"
- "Upsell packages automatically — spa, dinner, airport transfers"

### Dental / Medical Clinics

**Use case:** Appointment booking, treatment pricing, insurance questions, emergency inquiries

**Sample conversation:**
> **Patient DM:** "How much does a root canal cost? Do you accept insurance?"
> **AI Reply:** "Thanks for asking! At Elite Dental Clinic, root canal treatment starts at ₹5,000 per tooth. We accept all major health insurance plans and offer easy EMI options. Would you like to book a consultation appointment? Our dentist can assess your tooth and provide a detailed estimate."

**Lead capture triggered:** Pricing question, appointment inquiry, insurance question
**Follow-up:** Send appointment booking link, share doctor profiles

**Key selling points:**
- "Book more appointments automatically"
- "Answer pricing questions before the patient goes elsewhere"
- "Handle emergency inquiries instantly — direct to urgent care"
