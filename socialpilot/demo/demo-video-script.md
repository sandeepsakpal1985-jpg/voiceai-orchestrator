# MetaPilot AI — Demo Video Script (3 minutes)

**Target:** Small business owners
**Tone:** Exciting, simple, no jargon
**Style:** Screen recording with voiceover

---

## 00:00 — Hook (15s)

**Visual:** Split screen — small business owner looking overwhelmed at phone notifications vs. MetaPilot dashboard showing calm, organized leads

**VO:** "You're getting comments, DMs, messages from customers. You're losing leads, missing opportunities, and spending hours every day just to keep up. What if you could hire an AI sales employee — in 5 minutes — that does it all for you?"

**Text overlay:** "Hire an AI sales employee in 5 minutes."

---

## 00:15 — The Problem (20s)

**Visual:** Fast-scrolling Instagram comments, unread DMs, missed opportunities stack

**VO:** "Every comment on your posts, every DM from a potential customer — these are sales opportunities. But you can't be online 24/7. And hiring a human sales team? Too expensive, too slow."

**Visual:** Clock ticking, dollar signs flying away

**VO:** "MetaPilot AI changes that. It's your always-on AI sales employee."

---

## 00:35 — The Solution — Connect (30s)

**Visual:** Screen recording of onboarding — entering business name, clicking "Connect Instagram", OAuth dialog

**VO:** "Here's how it works. Connect your Instagram or Facebook business account — one click, no technical setup. Tell us about your business — what you sell, your tone, your pricing."

**Visual:** Knowledge base upload — drag and drop a pricing PDF

**VO:** "Upload your pricing, your product catalog, your policies. MetaPilot learns everything about your business."

---

## 01:05 — AI in Action (45s)

**Visual:** Split screen — real comment comes in on Instagram → MetaPilot detects it → dashboard shows it classified as "pricing question" → AI generates reply → auto-publishes

**VO:** "Now watch this. A customer comments 'How much does this cost?' on your post. MetaPilot instantly classifies it — pricing question, high intent — and generates a personalized reply using your actual pricing. It publishes the response naturally, just like a human would."

**Visual:** DM conversation flowing — customer asking questions, AI answering with personality

**VO:** "DMs? Same thing. Your AI sales agent handles the conversation, answers questions, and when it detects a hot lead — it creates a lead automatically in your CRM."

**Visual:** Lead card appears with score 85, warm category

**VO:** "Every qualified conversation becomes a lead. No manual data entry."

---

## 01:50 — Dashboard Overview (30s)

**Visual:** Dashboard walkthrough — leads page, CRM kanban, analytics charts

**VO:** "Your dashboard shows you everything: how many leads captured this week, which conversations need your attention, your deal pipeline moving through stages."

**Visual:** Insights page with AI attribution percentage

**VO:** "See exactly how much revenue your AI sales agent is generating. It's like having a sales team that works while you sleep."

---

## 02:20 — The Result (25s)

**Visual:** Before/After comparison:
- Before: 5 leads/week, missed responses, 2 hours/day on comments
- After: 50+ leads/week, 100% response rate, 10 minutes/day on approvals

**VO:** "Real results from our beta users: 10x more leads captured, 100% response rate, and they spend just 10 minutes a day reviewing AI-generated responses instead of hours on manual work."

**Visual:** Testimonial quote on screen

**VO:** "'MetaPilot handles my customer conversations while I focus on closing deals. It's like having a full-time sales team for the price of a coffee subscription.'"

---

## 02:45 — Call to Action (15s)

**Visual:** MetaPilot logo, "Get Started Free" button, 14-day trial badge

**VO:** "Ready to hire your AI sales employee? Start your free trial at metapilot.ai — no credit card required. Set up in 5 minutes, start capturing leads today."

**Text overlay:** "metapilot.ai — Start Free"

---

## Production Notes

- **Total length:** ~3 minutes
- **Music:** Upbeat, modern electronic (like the Stripe or Notion demo videos)
- **Pace:** Fast cuts, zoom transitions on screen recordings
- **Screen recording resolution:** 1440p or 4K
- **Captions:** Always on (increase retention by 40%)
- **End card:** Logo + "Get Started Free" + QR code linking to signup
