## ❌ Objection Handling

### "My team is small, we don't get many messages."
> "SocialPilot works best even with small volumes. It captures every lead, follows up automatically, and never misses a potential sale. Many of our customers with just 50 followers generate their first lead within 24 hours."

### "I already have a virtual assistant."
> "A human VA costs ₹15,000-₹25,000/month and works 8 hours a day. SocialPilot costs ₹499-₹1,499/month and works 24/7, never takes a day off, and handles Instagram + Facebook simultaneously. It's 10x cheaper and never sleeps."

### "Can it handle complex customer questions?"
> "The AI uses your FAQ and knowledge base to answer questions. For anything it's unsure about, it escalates to you for approval. You maintain full control while the AI handles the repetitive 85% of inquiries."

### "How is this different from a chatbot?"
> "Chatbots use rigid flows. SocialPilot is an AI sales employee — it understands context, remembers past conversations, detects buying intent, creates CRM deals, and drives revenue. It's not answering questions; it's closing sales."

### "What about data privacy?"
> "Your data stays in your own PostgreSQL database. AI processing uses OpenAI/Anthropic with strict no-training policies. We offer on-premise deployment for enterprise customers who need complete data control."

### "Do I need to connect my Instagram/Facebook?"
> "Yes, but it takes 2 clicks. We use official Meta OAuth — the same secure flow used by thousands of apps. The demo account shows exactly how it works without connecting anything."
